# The First Ascent: bounded content and honest duration

> **Historical scope record — superseded 2026-10-23:** The stop-expansion language and measurements below describe the earlier v27/Relay Works/Freight checkpoint, not the current work authorization. Subsequent authorized Rainline, Exchange, Freight, Warden and Cistern increments continue the meaningful-demo goal. Further bounded expansion is authorized as planned in `CAMPAIGN_EXPANSION_PLAN.md`; planned areas are not implemented. The no-padding and no-unmeasured-duration principles still apply. Preserve the historical text below as evidence, not as a current instruction to stop.


This is one finite, shorter mission, not the proposed full four-floor roguelite. It preserves the original city / arcology / eye-trade premise and adds an authored local ending: the evacuation lift is secured. It does not resolve the contradictory full-game endings or invent further sense systems.

## Duration target remains unmet
The initial twelve tiny sectors took a layout-aware input bot 82–90 simulated active seconds. They were replaced with six larger authored areas. The first revised retained-sight route took 138.03 simulated active seconds, defeating 31/42 enemies and finding 3/5 archives without dying. That evidence proves physical reachability, not a 30-minute human experience. The earlier 19–29-minute novice budget was unsupported and is withdrawn. Final measured routes are recorded in VERIFICATION.md; human first-play duration remains unknown. Archive reading and pauses are excluded from the active clock.

## Authored spaces
- Rainline District: city movement, service badge above the market, paired melee threats, optional field archive.
- Quarantine Intake: physical bolts, lower relay detour, guarded upper crossing and reflection opportunities.
- Exchange: optional sight trade, a safe calibration approach, ground-level props/listeners around the first relay, then an upper mixed-ranged crossing to the second relay. Refusing the trade remains viable.
- Freight Lock: direct loading-lane combat or an elevated ladder flank; use ordinary props tactically, then launch designated cargo with a real J strike into a supported receiver. Lost cargo can be replaced locally without healing or resetting enemies. Delivery permanently releases the physical interlock.
- Cistern: three-tier ascent, translating lifts, safe ladder alternatives, flood-control relay and optional archive.
- Relay Works replaces Listening Works: a safe hub, Service ground/upper loop, Lift switchback ascent and Dispatch ranged crossing. Three circuits remove real shutters and shorten return routes; direction signs aid recovery after a fall.
- Lift Warden: braced commitment, punishable recovery, parry/counter response, one lower ranged flanker in actual overlapping engagement height and a higher separate sentry.
- Above the Rain: brief explicit resolution, final statistics, restart/title.

## Bounded quality revision
Independent review identified that the original Exchange was only a single staircase and that Warden flankers were too high to engage the ground fight. The final focused edit makes lower/upper Exchange routes use distinct sound and combat opportunities, uses explicit prop heights, and lowers one Warden flanker. This improves authored decisions; it is not a claim to bridge two minutes of optimized traversal to thirty human minutes.

No extra floors, economy, random generation, other sacrificed senses, upgrade tree, online features, repeated-wave padding or inflated ordinary enemy health are added to chase the duration target.

## Final bounded expansion
After v27, one repeated relay area was replaced and one distinct physical-delivery area added. Further room stacking is out of scope. Acceptance covers the three circuit routes, direct/flank cargo approaches, genuine launch/contact/settling, recoverable cargo loss, both sight choices, checkpoint/retry/ending and native readability. Final four expanded input routes take 141.23–152.45 simulated active seconds. They finish all areas with 40–41 of 46 enemies defeated and 2–3 of 5 archives found. The thirty-minute target remains unmet.
