# Meaningful campaign expansion — design plan, not duration evidence

> Historical planning snapshot: Cistern and Upper Residential Crossing are accepted checkpoints. Relay Works districts are now accepted against the documented scope in WORKS_VERIFICATION.md. Their original proposed dimensions/counts below are retained as history, not additional content promises. No thirty-minute claim is established.

## Current gap
Seven gameplay sectors plus an ending currently contain about 21,800 pixels of combined horizontal footprint, 58 authored enemies, eight relay objectives, one physical cargo delivery and one intentional all-kills climax. Those counts do not measure distinct mechanics or enjoyable playtime. Optimized automation completes the Freight checkpoint campaign in 187.88–199.39 simulated seconds. It cannot establish how long an unfamiliar player takes, and no multiplier is applied.

The roughly 30-minute goal remains open. More connected authored traversal and decisions are appropriate; delays, forced waits, enlarged health pools and repeated waves are not.

## Next bounded increments
1. **Cistern journey:** investigate 3,200–3,800 pixels and drainage approach, maintenance interchange and upper spillway. Reuse the existing single flood relay. Provide lift-assisted and fixed ladder/platform approaches, a cache detour and a shorter opened return. Start with seven existing enemies on reachable fixed supports. First prove diagonal support transport and boarding/departure onto stationary landings. A moving-to-moving transfer may be optional, never the sole route.
2. **Upper residential crossing:** investigate a new 4,500–5,500-pixel district, with service court, overlapping housing roofs and exposed transit connection. Different routes change enemy exposure, noise and recovery opportunities. No additional relay, cargo delivery requirement or kill gate. Audit all sector-index assumptions before inserting a chapter.
3. **Relay Works districts:** investigate 6,000–7,000 pixels of service, lift and dispatch branches while retaining three existing circuits. Branch order, physical cross-connections and cache recovery should matter. Each branch needs a distinct traversal problem and recognizable landmark.
4. **Continuity:** align entrances, exits, signage and progression. Keep Warden a short target-order climax and the ending explicit.

These are planned scopes, not implemented features or promised timing. Freeze each increment for route/recovery tests, native readability and usability screening before the next.

## Evidence needed
- Both approaches and sight states at 60/120 Hz, with actual inputs and no outcome setters.
- Shared support/collision behavior, missed boarding/departure, falls, reachable displaced enemies, pause/death/retry/checkpoints.
- Native clips of meaningful decisions and recovery, not only successful fastest paths.
- A fresh-route AI screen can detect misunderstood cues but is explicitly not human validation. Record tool latency and assistance separately.
- Unfamiliar human play sessions must record active play, reading/planning, optional exploration, retries and inactivity separately before making a typical first-play duration claim. See FIRST_PLAY_CHECKLIST.md.

Stop expansion to repair any unrecoverable pocket, inaccessible required enemy, ambiguous objective, persistent blind-navigation failure or broken checkpoint. Do not count confusion as desirable duration.
