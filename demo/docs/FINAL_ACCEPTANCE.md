# Expanded demo acceptance

This is the bounded final slice after the preserved v27 checkpoint. Seven authored areas and an explicit eighth ending stage. The roughly 30-minute target is not achieved or validated. No further rooms, waves or health inflation are being added to manufacture duration.

## Player activities
1. City: learn connected movement/air jump, fight street guards, reach the elevated service badge and optional archive.
2. Intake: deal with physical bolts, restore a lower relay and take the guarded elevated crossing.
3. Exchange: freely retain or trade sight; ground listeners/props teach sound consequences before upper mixed combat.
4. Freight Lock: choose a direct loading lane or ladder flank, then actually strike cargo into a receiving basin. Support/contact/settling, not an E marker, opens the interlock. E replaces lost cargo without restoring health/enemies.
5. Cistern: climb three tiers, use moving lifts or ladder alternatives, restore flood control and recover an optional archive.
6. Relay Works: navigate Service, Lift and Dispatch branches; each circuit opens an actual shutter and shortens return routes. Ground signs and tested recovery ladders support returning after a fall, including the far side of a still-closed shutter.
7. Warden: exploit committed windups, braced attacks, counter/parry and recovery while one ranged flanker can overlap the fight.
8. Ending: enter the secured lift, see results and return to title.
