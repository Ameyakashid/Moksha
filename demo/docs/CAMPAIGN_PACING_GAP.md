# Campaign content and pacing gap — Rainline checkpoint

The roughly thirty-minute first-play target remains unverified. Route automation establishes that objectives can be completed, not how an unfamiliar player discovers, understands or enjoys them. Do not multiply bot times to invent human duration.

Current authored data contains57 enemies, five optional caches, eight relay activations, one physical cargo-delivery objective and one combat-clear finale, followed by an explicit ending. Those counts are not counts of distinct puzzles. The eight relays share one interaction family; most ordinary encounters reuse three enemy policies.

Meaningfully different demands currently include optional sight-for-speed information trade, actual cargo launch/contact/settling, timing and transferring between moving supports, Warden protection/recovery commitments, and route/resource choices. Rainline's roof path reaches the optional cache while the street path skips it and meets different threats.

Editorial traversal groupings (not engine mechanics): Rainline's three neighbourhoods; Intake's approach/control/crossing; Exchange's lower/upper calibration; Freight's loading approach/delivery; Cistern's three ascent legs; Relay Works' three connected circuits; Warden's arena approach. Adding platforms, guards or identical relay objectives alone would not establish meaningful length.

Next isolated authorized revision is Exchange circulation: consequential physical connections, lift/stair choice and recovery using existing modules, without a larger footprint or altered enemy stats. It is not included in this Rainline checkpoint. Later candidates are a Freight acquisition/transport/staging journey with one receiver, and a recoverable two-loop Warden arena emphasizing threat order rather than HP/waves. These are proposals, not completed features in this ZIP.

Evidence needed to assess first-play pacing: unfamiliar play sessions with active traversal/combat, discovery/decisions, optional exploration, retries, pauses and tool latency recorded separately. Engine videos here show scripted controller/proximity-input runs and do not replace that evidence. No artificial waiting or duration padding is used.
