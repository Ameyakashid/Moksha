# Unfamiliar-player validation checklist

Use the complete campaign from F5/New Mission, not a scripted route fixture. Do not promise a duration in advance or direct the player along the automation's path. Record build hash, hardware, input method and whether the player already knows the controls/levels.

## Record separately
- Wall-clock start/end and completion/abandonment point.
- Active traversal/combat time versus reading, planning and optional exploration.
- Death/retry counts and time lost to retries.
- Pauses, technical interruptions and remote-input/tool latency (exclude these from playable pacing).
- Which route and sight choice the player selected, optional caches found and objectives misunderstood.

## Observe without coaching
1. Can the player distinguish decorative hillside buildings from physical platforms and identify ladder access?
2. Do market/roof paths feel different in threats and resources, rather than one simply being longer?
3. Does "badge relay" explain what E activates and why the physical shortcut changes?
4. After a missed jump, can the player find a way back without retrying?
5. Does the sight-trade choice explain its cost and benefit, and can either choice complete the route?
6. Can the player infer which physical connection each Exchange relay opens and use the lift or fixed route deliberately?
7. Does cargo behavior communicate the Freight objective without hidden reset requirements?
8. Are Warden vulnerability and target-order decisions readable? Is the ending explicit?

After play, ask which decisions were meaningful, where progress became unclear, and which sections felt repetitive. Record answers rather than interpreting every delay as desirable duration. A successful automation run is not a substitute for these observations. Multiple unfamiliar players are needed before describing typical first-play pacing; never apply a guessed multiplier to bot timing.
