# Experimental campaign irritant adapter

This checkpoint ports the independently audited 11f1160 controller/source into a
new ee725ef campaign fork, with explicit sensory and owner lifecycle adapters.
This descendant adds three experimental placements; see ENCOUNTERS.md. No smell choice, immunity earned
through hearing loss, save/schema modification, or accepted-branch integration.

## Ownership
- `combat_pc`: unchanged audited admission, bounded attack/guard interruption and
  actor-wide rearm. No movement stun, damage or hidden slowdown.
- `simulation`: unchanged bounded source/profile/view; no campaign dependencies.
- `campaign/logic/irritant_disperser.gd`: owner/session/room/state/pending fences,
  proximity arming and explicit warning/hiss events. First approach within 300
  world pixels starts a full 1.2-second warning. Repeats every 0.4 seconds during
  warning/cloud. When out of range, disarms on recovery entry, not after the whole
  recovery timer; approaching again starts a fresh warning. Only host.player is
  a target. Enemy hearing still receives actual emitted world events.
- Campaign sonar: real existing propagation with temporary gas identity labels,
  player-range admission and live source/phase checks; no added gas geometry.
  Historical contour waves retain normal expiry. Blind never sees colored cloud
  or hardware. Sighted-deaf retains field presentation but no world audio/sonar.
- Campaign audio: original deterministic two-tone warning and filtered hiss,
  generated by `audio/generate_irritant.py`, with existing hearing gate. New
  kinds cannot fall through to STEP. No claim of human audio-mix acceptance.

NoiseBus callbacks are synchronous. The adapter emits only at the end of its tick
or immediately before returning from first arming, not inside the base field
phase callback. Consumers independently revalidate owner and phase before use.

## Checks and limits
`godot --headless --path . --script res://campaign/quality/irritant/sensory_contract.gd -- --hz=60`
Repeat with `--hz=120`. Use isolated HOME and XDG directories. Fixture disables
campaign saves and deliberately seeds positions/state; it is NOT natural play.
26 checks cover initial proximity warning, dedicated audio identity, real sonar,
blind/deaf gates, loading/pending/modal/session invalidation, synchronous event
invalidation and expired labels. This README describes the adapter gate; descendant encounter checks and their
remaining native/audio/human limits are documented separately in ENCOUNTERS.md.

The current `tools/package_nov6.py` runtime selector excludes any path component
`quality`, including this fixture and the original simulation/quality arena.
The original arena is not copied into this branch. Selector verification is NOT
a new PCK/export audit: no build or shipping acceptance is claimed.
