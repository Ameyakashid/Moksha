## What changed

returns, an optional service-index shelf, an upper gallery and a hazardous lower
aisle. The same objective IDs, dependencies, seven-enemy mix and reward identity
remain. Isolation now finishes beside east dispatch rather than sending the
west-first player back through the gallery after collecting both accounts.

Original local drawer banks, inhabited counters and rewriting machinery replace
the rejected panorama-crop collage. The original deep background remains intact.
Scenery has no input, collision, save or simulation authority. See the design and
atlas documents for original-art provenance and architectural reference mapping.
