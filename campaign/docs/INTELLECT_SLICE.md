# Intellect companion slice

A small original cognitive mote and restrained text reactions support the opening level. A delayed reminder points to the badge relay above hillside homes only while it remains unlit and progress has stalled; delivery waits for a quiet moment. Clinic guidance observes the actual opened shortcut. Other lines respond to existing actions, and a later real sight-station choice has retained/traded variants.

Normal notices, interaction prompts, chapter cards, menus and combat take priority. The mote is cosmetic, not a collision body or enemy detector; it disappears when sight is traded. The companion cannot change health, objectives, enemies, saves or controls. Chatter memory lasts for the current session, survives district retry and resets on New/Continue. No intellect sacrifice, voice acting, new canonical history or full crew-dialogue system is claimed.

Some lines may never find a suitable quiet window on a fast route. Native evidence separately verifies the visible opening reminder; synthetic eligibility and headless route receipts are not proof every line was painted.
