Next ownership step: detached player assembly
Baseline: fe9936a. Implemented as a bounded two-phase helper.

Decision
Extract one reusable original-code helper, demo/assembly/player_assembler.gd,
with two explicit static operations:
  configure_actor(player: CharacterBody2D) -> void
  attach_components(player: CharacterBody2D, health: Node) -> void
The mission retains PlayerScene.instantiate(), assignment to its player field,
the virtual health-construction hook, and the sole activation point. A two-phase
helper is deliberate: health selection currently happens AFTER profile/visual
configuration, while the host's player field is already assigned. A one-call
factory returning the player would change that observable hook contract or need
a callback/service locator. Avoid both. No factory holds the campaign host,
SceneTree, NoiseBus, room, save store, district definition or runtime state.

Existing sequence and proposed ownership (same sequence)
1. Host retires room, clears enemy/noise state, chooses district and builds room.
2. Host instantiates and assigns preserved PlayerScene.
3. Helper configure_actor sets Player name, manual physics, duplicated movement
   and combat profiles, shared chain, optional wireframe script and presentation.
4. Host calls _create_player_health(): demo original scene or campaign BuildHealth
   with the campaign depletion listener connected before DefeatResponse binds.
5. Helper attach_components names/configures Health and appends it, DefeatResponse,
   mission NoiseRelay, BodyShape/IncomingStrikeReceiver, ContactConfig,
   PhysicalImpactReceiver, BoundaryReceiver and ProjectileReceiver in EXACTLY
   current construction order and parent paths. Health maximum stays 60 initially.
6. Host room.add_child(player) activates the complete graph. Host resolves relay
   and defeat by their stable child names, binds bus, resets spawn/facing, captures
   base movement, applies trade, then connects its weak-reference defeat listener.
7. Host creates enemies/props and binds sonar, camera, gate and checkpoint. Campaign
   still adds objectives, captains and presentation and applies build afterward.

Why this is meaningful rather than directory parity
Player component selection and dependencies become inspectable/reusable without
constructing a room or run. The game-layer assembly recipe no longer hides among
level progression and saving. The existing demo remains the lifecycle facade;
the actor/controller and simulation components still own all behavior. This does
NOT complete district assembly or remove the host's remaining responsibilities.
No new generic ECS, global registry, new activation API or packed-scene inheritance.
A static helper is preferable to a new PackedScene here: health is selected by the
host, visuals are optional, and child readiness/connection order must be explicit.

Inspected reference mapping
shapez GameCore.initializeRoot constructs domain services; postLoadHook announces
post-load and then sets gameInitialized. EntityManager.registerEntity indexes
already-present components and sets registered before dispatching entityAdded.
Borrow the construction-before-publication responsibility, not implementation,
component model or factory-game systems. Godot already supplies instantiated
scenes, parent-owned lifetime, resource loading, child readiness and tree entry.
Our helper maps to game-specific composition; room.add_child maps to publication.
These are analogous responsibilities, NOT equivalent engine execution semantics.

Risks and preservation gates
- Child construction order is NOT readiness order: BodyShape already exists;
  its incoming receiver readies before later player siblings. Incoming/projectile
  receivers wait for actor.ready; ContactConfig also defers its application.
  Health and DefeatResponse bind during child readiness. Preserve all paths and
  original _ready ordering rather than moving binds into the assembler.
- Movement/combat resources must remain duplicated per actor. Shared read-only
  chain/presentation resources must retain their current sharing semantics.
- Hook call must see the assigned, configured but detached actor, and adding the
  complete actor must happen once. No component bind/reset or signal publication
  in the helper; no hidden game services or live-tree mutation.
- Test detached recipe directly: not inside tree, exact child topology/order and
  resource identities; mutate one actor profile and prove second actor unaffected.
  Trace hook state and activation callbacks in demo and campaign hosts.
- Compare a baseline/new topology and activation-order trace; confirm all health
  consumers bind same authority, exactly one damage/death delivery and unchanged
  campaign depletion callback order. A deliberate child-order/resource-sharing
  mutation must fail a focused assertion before reporting the test meaningful.
- Run existing health assembly, incoming real-contact, live build, save-feedback
  and adversarial save fixtures at 60/120 Hz with isolated HOME/XDG directories;
  retain 32-district smoke and content/domain contracts. No native desktop while
  ongoing video owns it; native verification is a later separately stated gate.
- Diff combat_pc/ and simulation/ bytes against frozen commit: zero. No content,
  stable IDs, save schema, spawn/reset, contact or defeat semantics changes.
- Keep readable Godot declarations and one operation per line in the new helper.
  Do not reformat unrelated host/controller files. If focused gates expose a
  semantic dependency, stop and report rather than widen this extraction.

Observed baseline details
Campaign adds CampaignOperativeSkin after actor.ready and duplicates its melee
chain during build application. Tests preserve both; initial detached assembly
shares the chain, while movement/combat profiles are duplicated per actor.
