Base: `891f781894e33b66ab1f9935e2acab5030393777`. One district only.
This is an implementation candidate, **not engine-, route- or native-accepted**.
The author did not launch Godot while the smell/native reviewers owned it.
Do not merge, package or infer playability from the static receipt alone.

## Player-visible change and stable meaning

`works_memory_loom` remains 04.4, between `works_cargo_exchange` and
`works_service_refuge`. Five levels / 32 districts are unchanged. The old pair of
similar very tall record stacks becomes a central public records commons, a low
listening archive to the west and an exposed rewriting gallery to the east.
Either record may be recovered first. Its own shutter then opens a different,
shorter ground return to the commons. Both records still preserve contradictory
testimony rather than resolve the original story's loop/immortality ambiguity.

| Stable objective | New position | Unchanged meaning/dependency |
|---|---|---|
| `first_account` | (620,300) | Independent recover, operative's account |
| `other_account` | (3360,80) | Independent recover, witness's account |
| `preserve` | (1960,300) | Install, requires both accounts, unlocks exit |
| `index` | (560,80) | Optional recover, vitality reward |

The reward/receipt key remains `works_memory_loom/index`. Labels, kinds, ordering,
prerequisite graph and reward semantics are preserved. The district checkpoint
still restarts its local goals; it does not serialize arbitrary actor coordinates
or opened shutters. Old/current supported envelopes retain the same district ID,
run identity and prior receipts. No save codec, version, reward or progression
code changes. Continue compatibility is a **pending runtime gate**, not a claim
proved by matching IDs.

Footprint: 5600 -> 3900 pixels; highest required support: -250 -> 80. Spawn is now
(1900,300), station (1800,300). Seven enemies keep the same mix: three listeners,
two sentinels, two sentries. Six physical props remain. No pits, electrical floor
strips, moving lift dependence, additional objective, all-kill/wave/time gate,
health inflation or changed motor. A smaller footprint does NOT certify a shorter
complete walking route: central comparison deliberately revisits the commons,
then the final eastward departure reuses the opened ground route. If that final
reuse feels like busywork in live review, simplify this candidate before acceptance.

## Concrete spatial/encounter beats

1. **Commons, x1680..2140.** Entry, existing station and comparison desk are
   separated physically. Recessed public-copy counters, restrained archive neon
   and eight additional decorative residents identify a place, not a diagram.
   Residents never collide, rescue, react to goals or emit life/supply scent.
   No enemy is authored in the entry pocket, but surviving ordinary guards are
   not given a new invisible leash and can pursue through an opened return.
2. **West listening archive.** Climb x1640 from 300 to 80, cross the solid bridge
   at x1320..1560, and descend x1160. A sentinel at (1280,80) guards the approach.
   Listeners at (1050,300)/(850,300) share a low aisle with physical props at
   (970,300)/(700,300). The first account is (620,300). Prop strikes/noise and
   ordinary close combat are existing mechanics, not new distraction scripts.
3. **West optional pocket.** A separate fixed ladder x460 reaches the shelf
   x380..880 at y80. Index (560,80), prop (660,80), listener (760,80). This shelf
   is not a required high-speed gap jump from the entry bridge. Falling from
   either shelf lands on continuous ground and a fixed ladder remains available.
4. **East rewriting gallery.** Climb x2160, cross x2240..2480 at y80. Real solid
   28x45 cover at x2650 and x3260 shields the near/far ladder approaches from
   sentries at (2810,80)/(3110,80). Each cover top is y35; a normal held jump can
   nominally clear 45px (base ideal apex about 60.6px), with no gap underneath.
   Actual clearance, enemy response and approach timing remain unverified.
   The witness is (3360,80); far fixed ladder x3440 is beyond the far cover.
5. **Optional ground sterilization aisle.** Descend x2560, cross x2880 beneath
   the gallery, ascend x3440. Existing default irritant at (2880,268), sentinel
   (2920,300), props (2710,300)/(3120,300). Exposure is the actual 120x64 volume
   x2820..2940/y236..300. The y80 gallery is outside it. The disperser does not
   directly damage health or block movement, but coughing can interrupt guard
   and attacks during ordinary melee. Smell sacrifice gives existing cough
   immunity, not electric/projectile/contact immunity. No new status tuning.
6. **Real returns.** West door (1440,205), east (2360,205), both 28x190, meet
   solid bridge undersides at y110 and ground at y300. They block the ground
   before their own record and leave the y80 outbound bridge usable. After that
   record, the standing-body ground corridor has no remaining authored solid.
   The west return goes directly to commons. East can return upstairs to x2560
   before descending, avoiding gas, or take the shorter lower aisle. Final
   preserve opens (3690,160), 28x280, before exit (3855,300).

No guard is authored within 100px horizontally of a same-height ladder endpoint.
This is a spawn-placement constraint, not proof enemies will stay there. Closed
shutter collision/line-of-sight can be inspected in the blockout; **representable
independent door graph and clear rectangles are not verified actor passage**.

## Wayfinding and supported-state fallbacks

Ordinary world signs identify both commons ladders, the first-account descent,
optional index ladder, gas-free upper gallery, lower sterilization aisle, ground
return conditions and comparison/exit. The HUD hint describes either branch
first and the separate optional ladder. Existing physical objective contours,
ladder contact feedback, sonar and objective HUD remain authoritative. Decorative
frontage adds identity, never the only clue or an invisible navigation permission.

- Neutral, slow Strength and Agility use the same fixed ladders and continuous
  ground. No speed augment, air-jump gap, wall skill, prop placement or lift phase
  is required. Upper cover asks for a short ordinary jump; the ground aisle and
  far ladder are an alternate traversal, not a trap. It is not damage-free while
  a live sentinel is present. Bad drops have ground and ladder recovery.
- Retained sight/hearing: signs, silhouettes, existing audio/telegraphs.
- Sight traded, hearing retained: ordinary scenery is hidden with its parent;
  Q/actual noise reveals physical ladders/cover/objectives. No exact beacon or
  invented smell navigation. Blind coverage must include real climbs/cover/drop.
- Hearing traded, sight retained: visible ladders/signs and existing warnings;
  no route depends on hearing the gas. Existing FOCUS remains unchanged.
- Smell retained: `scent_observer.register_room()` already registers optional
  recover goals with a nonempty reward. Thus the existing vitality `index` is
  eligible for the coarse **supply** category. The observer suppresses completed,
  already-earned and unavailable rewards. At the west account the index is about
  228px away, within its existing 300px supply range. This is generic reward
  supply evidence, NOT a record-provenance smell, exact objective beacon or new
  source type. No extra cache/reward was invented to manufacture a scent cue.
- Smell traded: that aid is gone; the permanent west ladder, sign, HUD hint and
  physical objective contour still work. Genuine immunity changes the lower
  encounter choice. Smell is independent of the supported vision/hearing states.
- Combined sight+hearing loss remains barred by the existing normal UI. A seeded
  state must not be advertised as newly supported navigation.

## Verification and acceptance plan

Engine-free check:

```
python3 campaign/quality/memory_loom_candidate/check_static.py --report /fresh/path/static.json
```

The literal parser executes no GDScript. It protects unaffected district text and
authority modules, stable signatures, same enemy mix, supports/ladder clearance,
return-door/ceiling geometry, exposure separation, actual scent eligibility and
view ownership. Nine negative controls include a fake blocked return, upper gas,
renamed ID, dependency coupling and endpoint ambush. It is not a Godot parser.
`geometry_diff.json` and `geometry_review.svg` are generated blockout review aids,
**not engine screenshots**. The SVG shares a scale for the before/after geometry.

Prepared but **not run**: `memory_loom_candidate/contract.gd` checks the actual
catalog and presentation lifetime/culling/sight boundary. Existing irritant
`encounter_contract.gd` now names the four exact districts rather than asserting
three: the three previous placements are unchanged; Memory Loom is the fourth.
Old three-encounter historical reports remain untouched and do not cover this one.

After the engine lease is free, import and run the new composition contract,
existing layout support/prose/catalog tests, Market habitat and irritant contracts.
Then use `acceptance_routes.json` for **ordinary-input** west-first/east-first,
upper/lower, optional-collect/skip runs. The detailed waypoints and observation
points distinguish closed bridge crossing, record interaction and actual open
return crossing. Test 60/120Hz, live enemies, retry after either record, Continue
from the stable district checkpoint, no repeated index reward/heal, and departure
to Service Refuge. A seeded diagnostics matrix covers three lenses x three normal
vision/hearing states x two smell states; it is NOT earned-choice evidence.
The listed acceptance runs require actual sense choices and a real committed
checkpoint, not a directly set immunity flag.

Native review must follow those same routes in motion, including blind/nonsmell
index access, bad-drop recovery, gas-free fallback, pause/retry and readable combat
against archive frontage. No frozen-AI beauty frame substitutes for traversal.
Record source fingerprints and actual unedited engine logs/footage. No human
playtime, Windows, native readability, full campaign completion or release
readiness claim is made by this candidate.
