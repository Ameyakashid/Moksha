One-way drop and dive now join the same actor through the action and collision-lifetime contract. Gated wall-run now joins the same action/weapon permissions and swept collision. Optional high-fall recovery and the ordinary slope-contact correction are implemented, with native hill traversal and paired-rate physical acceptance. Ladder/vine traversal now uses an independent editor-placeable sensor with its own ability gate. The pending surfaces each need their own source/behavior contract and fixture. No unrelated platformer powers belong in this PC.
