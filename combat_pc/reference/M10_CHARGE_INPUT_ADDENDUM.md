# M10 addendum: held charge, repeat and facing

Research date: 2026-10-04. Source pin: [N3rdL0rd/DeadCellsDecomp `77c3ad263c4187ade6d048ba77bb61f79e6cec44`](https://github.com/N3rdL0rd/DeadCellsDecomp/tree/77c3ad263c4187ade6d048ba77bb61f79e6cec44), identified by its README as PC v35 / build `50edff4`. This extends [the melee audit](MELEE_COMBO_CHARGE_EVIDENCE.md) and [the facing audit](FACING_AIR_CONTROL_EVIDENCE.md) only where needed for M10.

**Finding:** official evidence supports Katana's slash-to-held-dash branch, optional held chaining, and a fix preventing Katana charging while a shield is held. The pinned source preserves one meaningful Katana interruption dispatch, but no charge scheduler. It does not resolve release-to-execute versus automatic completion, early-charge timing, hold-repeat rearm or exact cancellation inputs. Those require explicit MOKSHA policy.

No runtime changes, measured gameplay timing or copied reference code/assets are included. The existing v13 light preset remains the baseline. [Machine-readable bounded facts](M10_CHARGE_INPUT_SOURCE_FACTS.json) contain source identities, confidence and unknowns.

## Primary developer evidence

- [Update 21][p21] introduces a standard slash followed by a charged dash while holding the attack button. The initial entry is dated **2020-12-03, alpha**; stable 21.4 is **2020-12-21**. This establishes a held branch after a slash. It says nothing about releasing to execute, automatic execution on reaching readiness, early release, repeat-after-dash or charge duration. Update 21.1 separately changes the third-strike control lock and repairs forward movement, without numeric rules.
- [Update 23][p23] says: **“Fixed being able to load the Katana's strike when holding a shield.”** The unwanted behavior was charging while shield was held. This supports rejecting that admission overlap. It does not specify raising a shield after charging has already begun, both buttons on the same tick, shield recovery, or whether a blocked held attempt can resume. Earlier audit shorthand about “fixing charge while shield held” must be read in this precise direction.
- The same [Update 23][p23] adds the optional **“Hold to chain attacks”** setting. It does not give a default, weapon exceptions, fresh-cycle rearm or precedence over Katana's native held-charge action. Initial 23 is **2021-03-04, alpha**; stable 23.8 is **2021-03-30**. A separate 23.5 note explicitly preserves dodge cooldown under held roll; that is not the missing melee-repeat scheduler.
- [Update 33.1, 2023-03-10, and 33.3, 2023-03-30][p33] restore Katana's cancellation technique and airborne technique respectively. They establish that those techniques were intended to remain available, but omit input recipes, phases, retained charge, release semantics and physics. No generic instant-cancel, hover or jump-refund rule follows.

These are historical statements, not measurements of the pinned v35 build. The official December 2020 Steam announcement also embeds Katana gameplay; the available web renderer could not display its GIF. No input trace or controlled visual measurement was obtained, so it cannot decide release versus automatic execution here.

## Selected representative MOKSHA policy

The first-example charge policy below is the **selected authored MOKSHA policy**, including its numbers and permissions. It is configurable behavior for one PC sample, not recovered Katana parity. The original MOKSHA goals favor responsive actions, independent speed/power tuning, physical contact and impact pauses; they do not require a stamina system or a weapon database.

1. **Separate input modes.** Preserve the v13 light chain in fresh-press mode. A repeat-enabled configuration derives at most one request at an admissible next-step boundary from the current held episode. An explicit configuration must decide whether a completed chain wraps while still held; this addendum does not infer that rule from Dead Cells. Repeat must use ordinary admission and reuse locks. A charge configuration owns the held input instead of also producing repeats. Reject or explicitly resolve ambiguous configuration; do not let one hold silently request both.
2. **Responsive lead-in and charge ownership.** A fresh admitted press starts the ordinary starter Slash. The same uninterrupted hold branches after **0.06 seconds of recovery**, once ordinary admission permits the action to yield and its old strike is retired. Releasing before that boundary leaves just the Slash. Charge time begins at the accepted branch, not during the preceding Slash. Early charge accumulation is off for this example; any later early mode must define its opening phase and clock separately.
3. **Explicit execution mode.** The selected first representative executes **one Charged Thrust automatically after 0.45 seconds of charging** while the same hold remains uninterrupted. Releasing during charge cancels it with no additional attack. Interruption also cancels it. Continued holding after execution cannot repeat or rearm the charge: release and a new admitted press are required. **0.06 and 0.45 seconds are authored MOKSHA tuning.** Release-after-readiness is a possible different policy, but is not the selected implementation. The reference evidence cannot choose between these trigger semantics.
4. **One held episode, no latent attacks.** Capture fresh edge, held state and release separately, tied to weapon/action generation. Completion or cancellation ends eligibility for that charged episode; a new charge needs release and a new admitted press. Cancellation, disabled controls, profile replacement and reset invalidate its pending requests. Holding through such an interruption must not manufacture a press or resurrect charging when permissions return. Repeat-derived requests are discarded on release; already accepted physical fresh presses retain the existing documented buffer policy.
5. **Facing without target seeking.** Allow manual turning during charge only when both profile and external permissions allow it. Charge completion starts the ordinary Thrust windup. Its allowed windup turning remains live; active entry commits contact-facing under the existing M9 step rule. Keep active geometry/impulse direction fixed for that strike lifetime. If actor turning later becomes permitted, it must not rotate an old sweep. This pass has no target query, target-conditioned airborne bonus, hidden aim candidate or jump/fall refund.
6. **Explicit cancellation and shield ownership.** Keep one weapon action owner. Prevent charging while the shield is active; preserve its existing cancellation gate and incurred locks. For the representative charge phase, an admitted shield/roll/jump transition cancels charge without generating a charged strike. Existing active-strike cancellation still follows its profile. Rejected transitions leave the old action intact. Simultaneous actions follow the existing actor arbitration, with at most one committed owner; this priority is MOKSHA policy. Do not imitate Katana's incomplete interrupt dispatch as an unconditional attack-on-cancel.
7. **Clock and contact discipline.** Charge uses the same documented pausable actor-time convention as other action clocks; hold/release intent can be captured during local hitpause without advancing charge. Keep readiness, control lock and reuse cooldown distinct. A charged execution creates one new strike identity, retires old sweep history and uses existing hit confirmation, noise, impulse and pause routing. Validate generation again after externally observable callbacks.

The selected numbers and automatic trigger belong only to this representative configuration; they do not change the preserved light preset or establish Dead Cells parity. No release-to-execute alternative is required in M10; unimplemented alternatives must remain visibly unsupported rather than appearing as functional settings.

## Focused passive-fixture checks

| Case | Required result for the chosen authored policy |
|---|---|
| Preserved baseline | v13 fresh-press light chain still needs independent presses; holding does not repeat or charge |
| Input ownership | Repeat-only hold produces admitted steps with no backlog; release stops derived requests; charge-only hold never repeats the ordinary chain concurrently |
| Charge boundary | Release before opening, before readiness, exactly at readiness and after readiness each have one documented outcome; a held lead-in does not pre-fill charge unless an early mode explicitly permits it |
| Execution and rearm | Selected automatic mode emits one Charged Thrust after its authored charge interval and stays blocked while held. A release during charge cancels; release/new press after completion starts a distinct episode. Define and test the same-tick release/readiness ordering |
| Shield/cancel overlap | Shield already active; shield raised during charge; both fresh presses together; nonholdable shield; existing shield lock. One owner commits, rejection is nonmutating, cancellation cannot produce hidden damage |
| Facing and lifetime | Reverse before commit, at commit and after commit; external turn denial; no retroactive sweep across the old direction or duplicate target contact |
| Pause and invalidation | Release/re-press during local hitpause; pause/resume; hurt/reset/disabled controls/profile replacement during callbacks. No stale charge, extra strike, renewed lock or replayed input |
| Ground and air | Start grounded, rising and falling; land during charge; moving support and wall proximity. Compare at 60/120 Hz using declared actor time; no new gravity, fall-risk, jump-budget or target-dependent behavior |

**Still unknown in Dead Cells:** release versus full-charge execution; charge thresholds/scaling; whether and how early charge overlaps a previous action; partial-charge outcome; hold-repeat default/cadence/wrap/rearm; charge-repeat precedence; action priority; all Katana cancel recipes and stored-charge rules; exact turn/aim sample time; phase movement and airborne physics; exact charge/cooldown/control-lock timing.
