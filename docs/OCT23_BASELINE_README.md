## Play

Import `project.godot` in Godot **4.6.3**. Press **F5**, then **Enter** for a new mission. **C** on the title resumes the last area checkpoint when one exists.

- **A/D / arrows:** move
- **Space / C:** jump; release for a shorter jump; press again for the air jump
- **J:** fresh presses chain Slash → Backhand → Finisher; holding does not auto-repeat
- **K:** raise for a timed parry, then hold to guard; a parried sentry bolt returns physically
- **Shift:** roll; a short interval is invulnerable
- **W/S:** climb a ladder; Space jumps off
- **S + Space:** drop through a thin platform; a fresh chord in air dives
- **Q:** tap the weapon to create a real-position sound pulse
- **E:** use a nearby relay, cache, station, or onward door; replace cargo at its cradle
- **R:** retry the current area; health, guards, relays, shutters and cargo reset
- **Esc:** pause; **M:** mute; **F12:** save a screenshot to Godot's user-data folder

Pause, archive and sight-choice panels freeze gameplay. The opening is skippable. The title is available from pause or defeat with **N**. There are no time-limit or repeated-wave gates.

## One complete mission

Seven authored areas lead to a short explicit ending:

1. Rainline District: city movement, service badge and street encounters
2. Quarantine Intake: ranged security, relay detour and elevated crossing
3. The Exchange: optional sight trade and maintenance listeners
4. Freight Lock: choose a loading-lane or ladder flank, then physically launch cargo into the receiver
5. The Cistern: vertical drainage route, ladders and moving lifts
6. Relay Works: three branching circuits with shutters that open shorter routes
7. Lift Warden: committed attacks, parries and ranged flankers
8. Above the Rain: enter the lift to finish and view run statistics

Field archives restore health once and provide short environmental notes. Each area starts at a checkpoint with full health. A retry preserves collected archives and the sight choice. Local checkpoint data and final statistics are stored in Godot's `user://` directory; there is no destructive save rule. Expanded checkpoint files use separate names so v27 saves remain intact.

## Trading sight

At The Exchange, E opens an explicit choice: **J** accepts, **K/Esc** refuses. Both routes can finish the mission. The trade grants +25% run/roll speed and stronger, longer sound traces. It persists for that mission, including retries.

Blindness covers the ordinary world. Real footsteps, strikes, enemy warnings, moving machinery and physical prop impacts reveal travelling white fragments of nearby collision surfaces. Silence fades them away. The connected player-body cue represents proprioception and reveals no terrain. Q helps map the next landing. This is a stylized distance-and-contour model, not physical acoustic occlusion or reflection simulation.
