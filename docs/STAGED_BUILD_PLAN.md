# MOKSHA build checkpoints

## Completed

1. **Independent art checkpoint.** Approved older operative, guard, urban kit,
   source sheets, original prompts, manifests and standalone art showroom.
   The original `moksha-assets` project remains separate and unchanged.
2. **Two placed rooms.** The approved next scope adds the city prologue and first
   arcology interior, with reusable editable terrain, actors, props and landmarks.
3. **Physics and control integration.** The tested foundation now uses the MOKSHA
   art, collision sizes, rigid-body prop noise/damage, intentional input buffering,
   attack cancellation and terrain-obstructed strikes.
4. **First sight trade.** One station gives the approved speed fallback and turns
   the world into black with real-origin travelling white contour echoes.
5. **Combined verification.** Actor, sensory, room, scene-reuse and run-flow
   regressions plus native visual/play checks. See tests/README.md for the limits.
6. **Playtest-driven polish.** More forgiving second jump, first-landing teaching,
   real-origin guard draw/strike warnings, positional sound routing, and clearer
   own-body/weapon feedback in the black view. Connected nine-ledge traversal is
   verified with live guards and props.
7. **First bespoke attack body sequence.** Six separate transparent drawings,
   measured grip/foot anchors, optional graphics profile and native study scene.
   Combat timings and collision stay owned by their original controller/resources.
8. **Blind contact readability.** Actual damage, break and defeat sounds have
   short distinct white confirmation. Nearby labels/glyphs are consolidated,
   interrupted warnings retire, and independent surface propagation is preserved.

## Not part of this two-level delivery

The opening spear-catch cinematic, other sense branches, strength/agility build
paths, four-floor progression, final boss, adaptive runs and ending decisions are
still future design work. The supplied notes preserve alternatives for reset
versus immortality lore, allies, the bomb and endings; this slice chooses none of
those alternatives. No destructive save behavior exists.

The next useful design review is the blind traversal/combat feel: pulse speed,
range/trails, noise density, the 25% speed reward and the simplified self-position
cue. Those values are independently configurable before adding more content.
