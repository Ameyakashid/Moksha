# Production cargo native review

Godot4.6.3,2026-10-19. An independent reviewer used ordinary keyboard input in
the rendered default mission through the supported whole-desktop CUA surface.
No debug state injection, teleport, forced outcomes or proof overlays were used.
The native sample preceded the final callback-diagnostic retirement, absent-meta
guard and authenticated-carrier-only narrowing. Those boundary fixes changed no
controls, motor, artwork or ordinary valid cargo presentation; the final sources
were subsequently rerun headlessly and independently. No new native coverage is
implied for those adversarial branches.

The editor initially displayed a stopped run's listener regression parse output
(`report_prefix`). Current source declares that inherited member. A fresh import,
both-rate listener173-check suites, and refreshed native default mission launch
were clean. After native stop, current Output showed0 errors/0 warnings; the
Debugger's retained count was21, matching the previously documented baseline.
Its retained entries were not exhaustively cleared/reinspected, so this is not
an assertion that the entire debugger history is empty.

## Observed

- Ordinary movement and jump displaced a raised crate. The crate became airborne,
  rotated and fell into a fight with a visible break effect.
- After a retry, ordinary J near a falling crate showed a strike/HIT and an intact
  rotated crate. Overlapping player-body and sentinel contact prevented reliable
  attribution of a charged launch or actor damage to that crate.
- No clipping or interruption was observed during this bounded review.
