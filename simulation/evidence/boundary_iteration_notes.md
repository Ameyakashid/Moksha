- Early import/class-cache and fixture setup corrections were resolved before the
  51-scenario production suite. The actual-return fixture required a genuine held
  jump rather than an immediate short-jump release; production outcomes were not
  assigned or contact thresholds changed.
- The world-space review legend clipped and overlapped the instruction banner.
  The final HUD legend was accepted in a separate native presentation check.
- Independent retired-packet probes found three freed-instance runtime errors:
  source/receiver type checks ran before validity, and cargo's typed authority
  argument rejected a freed reference before its body could guard it. There were
  no unauthorized effects. New public boundary entry points now reject stale
  Variants before type/member access. Exact red/green probes are retained with
  the independent report.
- An independent genuine-fall World2D swap-and-restore probe exposed a real stale
  lethal Health debit: final equality snapshots alone were insufficient. Source,
  receiver and BoundaryScope now invalidate synchronously on canvas/world changes.
  The unchanged probe and its original Health 0 failure are retained separately
  from its final Health 60 rejection.
- `boundary_unconfigured_xdg_*` logs record an invocation setup failure: Godot
  launched without writable sandbox XDG paths and aborted during startup. The
  configured runs use explicit writable /tmp data/config/cache paths. Those
  aborted invocations are not counted as tested gameplay or as clean passes.

Only the final frozen-source aggregate, independent green reports, and final
preservation/clean-import verification constitute acceptance. No previous ZIP or
historical report is replaced by these diagnostics.
